About Morden Tourist

Pioneering immersive island travel, tailored holiday experiences, and world-class service from the heart of Chandigarh.

Our Legacy & Vision

Crafting Unforgettable Journeys Since Inception

Founded with a passion for transformative travel, Morden Tourist (operating via mordentourist.com) was established in Chandigarh to bridge the gap between ordinary holiday sightseeing and truly personalized luxury exploration.

Inspired by the architectural and natural magnificence of global archipelagos — such as the awe-inspiring island wonders of Abu Dhabi, the pristine turquoise waters of Lakshadweep, and the tropical rainforests of the Andaman Sea — we curate trips that allow travelers to slow down, connect deeply with nature, and revel in effortless comfort.

Our Mission

To deliver seamless, transparent, and exhilarating travel experiences that create lifelong memories for every traveler.

Our Vision

To be Northern India's most trusted, innovative, and customer-centric travel management company for island and luxury tourism.
